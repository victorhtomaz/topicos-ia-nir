 Near Infrared Spectra of Origin-defined and Real-world Textiles (NIR-SORT):
   A spectroscopic and materials characterization dataset for known 
   provenance and post-consumer fabrics

<<>><<>><<>><<>><<>><<>>| Benchtop_N NIR Spectroscopy |<<>><<>><<>><<>><<>><<>>

File naming convention augmentation for standoff distance
     Distance is encoded with replicate number: _XYZ
     where X = distance, Y = replicate, and Z = absorbance or reflectance
         3 = 0.5 cm
         4 = 1 cm
         5 = 2 cm
         6 = 3 cm
         7 = 4 cm
         8 = 8 cm

     Note: There is no 'zero distance' file for instrument Q. This is 
     equivalent to the file with the same sample ID under instrument N. 

Sample Prep and Measurement
     Each fabric specimen, approximate dimensions of 10 cm by 10 cm, was folded 
     such that a total of six layers of fabric were stacked and positioned on 
     the sampling window. However, similar reflectance counts can be achieved 
     with one layer of fabric by covering the specimen with a backing material 
     (see Data Descriptor publication for more details). For size-limited 
     specimens, i.e., those unable to create six layers to cover the 
     integrating sphere spot size, a cap lined with aluminum foil was placed 
     over the single layer specimen.
	
     Each replicate was measured on nonoverlapping weft and warp yarns 
     such that each measurement is an independent sampling of the yarns, 
     i.e., no two measurements contain the same set of yarns.
